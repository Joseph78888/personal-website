# Project presentation mockups

These generated concept interfaces illustrate documented workflows. They are not production screenshots; screen values, names and photos are illustrative. NOOR remains in active development, Crop Care is an academic implementation, and Local Services Marketplace is a proposed MVP.

Each project has seven distinct images, with ten phone depictions. The case study follows the Tectonix fintech reference's sequence: a three-phone hero, a two-phone composition and single close-up in a pair, three individual phone views, then a final large phone view. Backgrounds and interface accents are neutral.

| Index | Composition | NOOR | Crop Care | Marketplace |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Three-phone hero | Customer, vendor, operations | Capture, result, history | Customer, provider, approval |
| 1 | Two overlapping phones | Browse and product | Capture and analysis | Discovery and provider |
| 2 | Single close-up | Product detail | Classification result | Provider profile |
| 3 | Single angled phone | Cart and COD checkout | History | Request details |
| 4 | Single phone on platform | Vendor preparation | Capture guidance | Provider requests |
| 5 | Single suspended phone | Delivery operations | Connected assistant | Job progress |
| 6 | Final single phone | Profile and orders | Profile and settings | Profile, jobs and reviews |

Presentation ratios: hero 2:1 (16:9 on mobile), paired and triptych images 4:3, final image 3:2. Image rows use 16px gaps and stack on mobile. WebP encoding preserves generated image content.

Reference: https://tectonix.digital/projects/fintech-app
